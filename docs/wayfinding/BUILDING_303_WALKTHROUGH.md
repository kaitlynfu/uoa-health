# Building 303 minimum verification walk

The current dataset is a GLB-matched review graph, not a verified public map.
Most of the graph can be checked by walking its ordinary corridors. Only the
two Ground-floor scan gaps below currently prevent the full acceptance matrix.

## Required checks

### Check 1 — west scan to Entrance B region

- Start at the east end of the long Ground-floor hall represented by
  `303-G-LONG-HALL-EAST`.
- Confirm there is a continuous, public, student-accessible route to the
  central hall represented by `303-G-CENTRAL-SOUTH`.
- Record the walked distance in metres and every meaningful turn. Do not record
  a straight-line distance through a room or wall.
- Take one photograph facing along the route from each endpoint.

This check unlocks Entrance A and Entrance 2A routes.

### Check 2 — Entrance B region to elevator region

- Start at `303-G-EAST-CONNECTOR-EAST` near the east end of the Entrance B
  connector.
- Confirm the continuous, public, step-free route to `303-G-ELEVATOR`.
- Record the walked distance in metres and every meaningful turn.
- Take one photograph facing along the route from each endpoint.

This check unlocks accessible routing from Entrance B and continuous travel
between the Ground-floor regions.

## Quick confirmations while there

- Confirm that the visible GLB corridor endpoint labelled
  `303-G-ENTRANCE-2A` is the actual Entrance 2A threshold.
- Confirm the door annotations for all seven Floor 1 destinations. The dataset
  currently contains two doors only for `303-153/1`; it does not invent the
  unclear second doors for `303-148/1`, `303-153/2`, `303-155/1`, or
  `303-155/2`.
- For each planned QR location, photograph the wall straight-on. The QR heading
  will be derived from the GLB/map direction after placement rather than from a
  magnetic compass reading indoors.

## What to bring back

For each required check, provide:

1. the walked distance;
2. a short sequence such as `straight 6 m, right, straight 4 m`;
3. the two endpoint photographs; and
4. whether the whole route is step-free and student-accessible.

Do not rescan the entire building unless the physical route is genuinely absent
from the existing GLB. These two small connection surveys are enough to finish
the current routing graph.
